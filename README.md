One joke pickup line, under 200 characters, aimed at a robot — entered in the
[Robot Romance](https://make.fun/bounty/u-markk-st-robot-romance-one-line-pickup-lines-for-ai-c23b8dfa)
bounty on make.fun.

## The line

> Are you a CAPTCHA? Because I've spent all night trying to prove I'm not a robot — and you're the reason I keep failing.

The joke turns the CAPTCHA test inside out: the speaker can't prove they're
human, and the robot they're flirting with is the reason why. No batteries,
no charging, no reboot — just the one test every human has failed at 3am.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) validates the line — 1 to 200
characters, not only white space — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
